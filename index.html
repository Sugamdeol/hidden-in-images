Hidden in Images

Hide a secret message inside an ordinary-looking picture, so only someone who knows to look will find it. Inspired by the Fable 5.1 steganography post: a picture that looks normal can carry a message.

The decoy caption is what the picture appears to be about. The real message is hidden in the pixels, not in the caption.

Your token is used only in your browser for that one call and is never sent to any server of ours. There is no server: this page runs entirely in your browser.

Use PNG. The download is a PNG because PNG is lossless. Saving as JPEG re-encodes the picture and destroys the hidden bits.

How it works

The message is written into the least significant bit of each red, green and blue pixel value. Changing a pixel from, say, 142 to 143 is invisible to the eye, but the pattern of those last bits spells out the message. A short header marks pictures made by this app, so reveal can tell a plain picture from a carrying picture.

Nothing is uploaded. Your picture and your message never leave your browser, except for the optional Pollinations calls you ask for: a cover picture from the Pollinations image model, and a decoy caption from a Pollinations text model.

Paying with your own Pollen: if you paste your own Pollinations token above, LLM calls are made with that token, so your own Pollen pays. Without a token the anonymous tier is used.
